Meet the Boys

From laid-back legends to scheming villains — every character in Dufftown is carrying something. Usually a slab.

🧔

Lez Mackerel

The Protagonist

Leslie "Lez" Mackerel is Dufftown's most legendary bogan — a middle-aged, foul-mouthed, perpetually-stubbie-holding larrikin with a heart of gold buried somewhere beneath several layers of flanno. He's laid-back to the point of horizontal, but somehow ends up at the centre of every surreal catastrophe the universe can throw at him.

"Noice. Smort."
🦶

Sassy the Sasquatch

The Best Mate

Sassy is Lez's best friend, housemate, and the creature responsible for one of the internet's most beloved battle cries. Despite being a large, hair-covered cryptid of unknown origin, Sassy fits right into Dufftown life — chilling out, drinking slabs, and letting rip with the signature call that launched a thousand memes.

"Badoing badoing badoing."
😤

Donnie

The Villain

Donnie is Dufftown's resident antagonist — a greasy, scheming, deeply unpleasant bloke who seems to exist purely to make everyone else's life difficult. He's got the charisma of a kicked bin and the moral compass of a broken crowbar, yet somehow keeps turning up like a bad five-dollar note.

"Get outta my pub, ya dog!"
🌟

Clarence

The Mystic Father

Clarence is Lez's ancient, otherworldly father — a mystical being of cosmic wisdom who speaks in cryptic pronouncements and appears to have transcended the normal limits of human understanding. He functions as the show's unlikely moral compass, dispensing profound life advice between sips of something deeply mysterious.

"The cosmos speaks, my son."
🎬

Tommy Wiseau

The Inexplicable Cameo

Nobody asked for this. Nobody expected this. Yet here he is — Tommy Wiseau, inexplicably wandering into Dufftown like he owns the place. What is he doing here? Why is he wearing that jacket? These questions will remain forever unanswered. It's The Big Lez Show. Just roll with it.

"Oh hi, Dufftown."
