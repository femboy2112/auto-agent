Origins

The Big Lez Show

The Big Lez Show is an Australian adult animated web series created by Jarrad Wright, who voices, animates, and writes the entire series himself. It ran from 2012 to 2019, producing five series plus specials, and it did all of this on a budget that could generously be described as "basically nothing."

The series follows Leslie "Lez" Mackerel — a foul-mouthed, ageing bogan in the fictional town of Dufftown — and his rotating cast of deadbeat mates. The animation is deliberately lo-fi, the humour is relentlessly surreal, and the voice acting sounds like it was recorded in a garden shed at 2am on a Tuesday. Which, reportedly, it was.

Despite (or because of) all this, the show developed a fiercely loyal cult following across Australia and beyond. Its characters speak in an exaggerated Australian vernacular that somehow became universally quotable, transcending the show itself to live on as memes, merch, and endless forum threads.

"Badoing badoing badoing" — the battle cry of Sassy the Sasquatch — is the show's most iconic export. Three words, zero context required, infinite comedic energy. Ya bloody beauty.

History

The Big Lez Timeline

From shed to legend — key milestones in the badoing journey.

2012
The Shed Opens

Jarrad Wright uploads the first episode of The Big Lez Show to YouTube. Lez Mackerel, Choomah Island, and an immediately iconic bogan aesthetic land in front of a very confused internet.

2013–2014
Sassy Enters the Scene

Sassy the Sasquatch is introduced and immediately steals the entire show. His debut badoing utterance reverberates through the lounge rooms of Australia and begins its slow, unstoppable migration to the internet at large.

2015–2016
Viral Badoing Moment

Clips of Sassy's "badoing badoing badoing" begin circulating on Reddit, Facebook groups, and Australian meme pages. The phrase detaches from its source material and becomes a universal reaction to chaos, excitement, or just general vibes.

2017
The Choomah War

Series 4 delivers the Choomah War arc — a full escalation into surreal cosmic horror lore that somehow makes Dufftown feel like genuine high fantasy. The fandom explodes. Badoing achieves peak cultural saturation.

2019
The Finale

The Big Lez Show concludes with its fifth and final series. Wright ends the story on his own terms. Fans mourn. The meme lives on. Sassy's big feet continue to stomp through the collective consciousness of Australian internet culture, forever.

Today
The Legend Endures

Years later, "badoing" still appears in comment sections, Discord servers, and group chats without any need for explanation. The highest honour a meme can achieve. Fair dinkum.
